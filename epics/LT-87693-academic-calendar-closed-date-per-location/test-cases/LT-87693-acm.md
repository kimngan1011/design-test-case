# Test Cases: LT-87693 — Academic Calendar Closed Date per Location

**Suite:** CRUD Academic Calendar Master (ACM)
**Qase suite:** PX > Master data > Academic Calendar > CRUD Academic Calendar Master (ACM)
**Epic:** [LT-87693](https://manabie.atlassian.net/browse/LT-87693)
**ACs covered:** AC 01.1, AC 01.2, AC 01.3, AC 01.4, AC 02.1, AC 03.1

**Precondition (all cases):**

- Feature flag `Enable_Enhance_Academic_Calendar` is enabled in the org
- At least one Academic Year (AY) record exists (e.g., **"AY 2025-2026"**)
- At least 3 active Locations exist: **"Center A"**, **"Center B"**, **"Center C"**
- One closed-down Location exists: **"Closed Center X"**
- Brand **"Brand A"** has two child centers: **"Brand A – Center 1"** and **"Brand A – Center 2"**
