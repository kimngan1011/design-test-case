# Test Cases: LT-87693 — Academic Calendar Closed Date per Location

**Suite:** CRUD Academic Calendar Individual (ACI)
**Qase suite:** PX > Master data > Academic Calendar > CRUD Academic Calendar Individual (ACI)
**Epic:** [LT-87693](https://manabie.atlassian.net/browse/LT-87693)
**ACs covered:** AC 02.1, AC 02.2, AC 02.3

**Precondition (all cases):**

- Feature flag `Enable_Enhance_Academic_Calendar` is enabled in the org
- ACM "AC Master 2025" exists for "AY 2025-2026" and has been applied to locations **"Center A"**, **"Center B"**, and **"Center C"**
- ACIs exist: **"AC Master 2025_Center A"**, **"AC Master 2025_Center B"**, **"AC Master 2025_Center C"**
- The ACM has terms: "Term 1" (Start: 2025-04-07, 12 weeks) and "Term 2" (Start: 2025-09-08, 12 weeks)
- The ACM has 2 cloned closed dates: **"2025-04-29"** (Showa Day) and **"2025-08-11"** (Mountain Day)
- Center Manager (CM) for "Center A" is a separate user account with access only to "Center A"

---

## Suite: CRUD Academic Calendar Individual (ACI)

### Academic Calendar Individual – View – After Clone from ACM – Info Matches ACM; Apply to Location Button Hidden

**Description:** AC 02.1 — CRUD Testing, Role-Based Access — Confirms that after cloning from the master, the ACI displays correct information (name, terms, closed dates) and the "Apply to Location" action is hidden.

| 5   | Look for an "Apply to Location" or "Apply to All Locations" button | The button is **not visible** on the ACI detail page                                                             |           |
